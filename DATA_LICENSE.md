# Data licence

The data produced in this study (the files in `data/` derived by `code/02_province_members.py`, all files in `results/`,
`figures/`, `manuscript/tables/` and `manuscript/supplementary_tables.xlsx`) are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

The derived files are based on third-party data, which keep the terms of their providers:

- SEAS5 seasonal forecasts: Copernicus Climate Change Service, licence of the Climate Data Store dataset
  "Seasonal forecast monthly statistics on single levels". Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service
  information 2026. Neither the European Commission nor ECMWF is responsible for any use that may be made of the
  Copernicus information or data it contains.
- CHIRPS v2.0: Climate Hazards Center, University of California, Santa Barbara.
- Niño3.4 and DMI: NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory, computed from HadISST1.1 (Met Office Hadley Centre).
- Province polygons: batas-administrasi-indonesia (Alf-Anas), compiled from the boundary data of the Ministry of Home
  Affairs of Indonesia.
